Who We Are - 123 Main Road, Rosebank

Jo's Bakery is a small local bakery at 123 Main Road, Rosebank, Pretoria 0181 that specialises in preparing and selling fresh, delicious and affordable baked products. Our product range includes cakes, cupcakes, pies, banana cake, cookies and bread. We aim to serve individuals, families and businesses by offering a variety of baked goods for everyday enjoyment and special occasions.

Located at 123 Main Road, Rosebank - Blue door 8a - 5 min walk from Rosebank College. Our story started in 2018-2020 as a family kitchen with Gogo's recipe book from 1970. What started with vetkoek sold for R10 in a Checkers bag to help pay for college has grown into Jo's Bakery EST 2019. Bread baked daily at 3am before the city wakes up.

The bakery aims to provide customers with quality products at reasonable prices while maintaining friendly and helpful customer service. Customers can contact Jo's Bakery to enquire about available products, prices and customised cakes for birthdays, celebrations and other events.

The website was developed to help Jo's Bakery establish an online presence at https://jos-bakery-rosebank-0181.netlify.app/. It provides visitors with information about the bakery, its products, contact details and enquiry options.
