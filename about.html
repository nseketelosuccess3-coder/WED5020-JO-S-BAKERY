Our Story - 123 Main Rd, Pretoria 0002

Jo's Bakery started in 2018 at 123 Main Rd, Church Square, Pretoria Central 0002. We are a family bakery that believes in fresh, affordable, delicious products for our community.

We wake up at 3am every day to bake fresh bread, cakes, pastries and pies. Our recipes are from our grandmother, passed down for 3 generations. We use local ingredients from Pretoria suppliers.
